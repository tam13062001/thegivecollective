2 · Scoring

Major donor if is at least
An active monthly donor scores at least F4 after 2 gifts and F5 after 3, so steady monthly supporters can reach Loyal donors and Champions. Donors who missed a payment are scored on their gifts only.
How the scores work

Each donor gets 1–5 on each dimension; 5 is best. Quintiles split donors into five equal-sized groups (ties stay together). Bands use fixed cut-offs, which suit frequency because most donors give only once. Segments come from the R and F scores, the standard nonprofit grid; the M score ranks donors within each segment.
